## 1. Job and audience

- **Surface:** Marketing site (B2B AI services). Pages: Home `/`, AI Solutions `/ai-solutions/`, Contact `/contact/`.
- **Audience:** Owners and operations leads at SMEs in Asia-Pacific; mobile-first browsing; secondary: technical evaluators.
- **Job:** Understand what Omnitron does in seconds. One action: "Start the conversation".

## 2. Outcome and proof

- Success: 10-second clarity, single obvious CTA, visitor knows what the company does.
- Copy carries summaries — not full-length explanations — so visuals own the weight.
- Visual storytelling (3D + scroll) must earn trust without text-heavy sections.

## 3. Selected direction

1. **Visual-first redesign.** Text-heavy → visuals-first. Summaries replace full explanations site-wide.
2. **Full-bleed retained.** Maximize screen space. Middle of bands occupied only by necessary elements.
3. **Minimal corporate look.** Applied site-wide. Keep depth/3D/parallax; system itself is minimal, corporate, visuals-led.
4. **Theme:** light / off-white dominant system. Palette constrained to white / off-white / blue / grey. Orange accent retired.
5. **3D direction:** orbit → workflow motif. Three.js utilization increased.

## 4. Scope and boundaries

In scope: Home, AI Solutions, Contact; five service lines; single contact path; honest proof slots.
Out of scope: cybersecurity / Intelligence & Advisory content; rebrand; old wording.

## 5. Constraints and open decisions

- Cards/borders: survive for now, may change later.
- Workflow: concept approved; per-service step chains to be finalized.
- Typography, final component treatments, workflow details: implementation-phase decisions.
